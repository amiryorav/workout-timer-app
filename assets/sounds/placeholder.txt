Audio files needed for the app:

1. exercise_start.mp3 - Sound played when an exercise starts
2. rest_start.mp3 - Sound played when a rest period starts
3. break_start.mp3 - Sound played when a break between sets starts
4. countdown.mp3 - Sound played for each count during the final 3-second countdown
5. complete.mp3 - Sound played when the workout is completed

You can download free audio files from sources like:
- https://freesound.org/
- https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
- https://www.zapsplat.com/

Or generate them using a text-to-speech service.

For testing, the app will function even without these audio files in place, 
with appropriate error handling to fail gracefully.
